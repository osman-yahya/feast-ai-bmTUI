Step 5 · export

Quantisation can undo the training

2,862 MBthe bundle
devchosen on, not tests
4-bit, one scale per row
4-bit, one scale per block of 32
Each group of weights is rounded to 16 levels around its own scale. Smaller groups, closer fit: 290 MB more.

The converter's stock recipe and channel-wise int4 each lost a third of the answers; 21 and 15 of their 200 calls could not be read by LiteRT-LM's own parser. Block-wise int4 (with int8 on the per-layer embedding projection) kept them.
